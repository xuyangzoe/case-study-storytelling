Story contract故事契约

Central question核心问题
How do you turn a monetization feature nobody could explain a real reason to use into something viewers actually wanted to do, live, in the moment? 当没有人能说清楚一个变现功能"为什么值得用"时,要如何把它变成观众在直播当下真心想做的一件事?
Recommended argument推荐的故事主张
By digging past the mechanic — a currency you spend — into the actual behavioral driver behind it — wanting to be seen supporting someone in front of a crowd — a stalled, five-times-rejected feature became a gifting system with a real reason to exist: one that shipped, launched to real transaction volume, and revealed that KICK's actual engagement currency was visibility, not value. 通过挖穿表层机制——一种可以花费的虚拟货币——找到其背后真正的行为驱动力——想在众人面前被看见自己在支持某人——一个卡壳、被五次否决的功能,最终变成了一套"真正有存在理由"的送礼系统:它成功上线、产生了真实交易量,也揭示出 KICK 真正的参与货币其实是被看见,而不是花费的金额。

Findings核心发现

  • The genuine low (five rejected pitches) and the workshop reframe are strong and well-supported by your own account — they should be the narrative spine, not the feature mechanics. 真实的低谷时刻(五版提案被拒)和工作坊里的重新定义,是你自己亲历且有力的素材——它们应该是故事的主干,而不是功能机制本身。
  • The strongest evidence you currently have is written product-spec documentation, not screenshots — nearly every slide still needs a visual artifact before this is presentable. 目前最扎实的证据是文字形式的产品设计文档,而不是截图——几乎每一页在可展示之前都还需要补充视觉素材。
  • There was no A/B testing process and no post-launch loop for changing a design based on how people used it. That's the honest frame for the closing lesson — and it means the "reshaped our priorities" claim on SLIDE-10 needs checking before an interviewer probes it. 团队当时没有 A/B testing 流程,上线后也没有根据用户实际使用情况修改设计的机制。这才是结尾教训的诚实前提——同时也意味着 SLIDE-10 里"重新调整了优先级"这个说法,需要在面试官追问之前先自行核实。

Kicks — KICK's Cheer & Gifting SystemKicks——KICK 平台的打赏与送礼系统

KICK's answer to Twitch's Bits — a virtual currency viewers buy and send to support streamers live. KICK 对标 Twitch Bits 的答案——一种观众可购买、用于实时支持主播的虚拟货币。

Role: Product Designer, solo — owned design end-to-end across web and mobile · Hero artifact needed: Cheer Panel (see ASSET-01) 角色:产品设计师,独立负责——端到端主导 Web 与移动端设计 · 需要主视觉素材:打赏面板(见 ASSET-01)
SLIDE-01stated-only仅口述
Viewers had no real-time way to show they backed a streamer — and KICK had no monetization engine built around that moment.观众没有实时的方式表达自己支持某位主播——而 KICK 也没有围绕这一时刻建立起变现机制。
Content内容
On KICK, viewers react to live moments in real time but had no lightweight way to make that support visible to the streamer or the room. For the business, that was also a gap: no revenue mechanic tied to genuine, in-the-moment engagement. Kicks set out to solve both at once — give viewers a way to cheer, and give KICK a new revenue stream built on real behavior, not a bolted-on payment button. 在 KICK 上,观众会对直播中的精彩时刻实时产生情绪反应,却没有一种轻量的方式,把这份支持可见地表达给主播和聊天室。对业务来说,这同样是一个缺口:没有与真实、即时参与绑定的营收机制。Kicks 想要同时解决这两个问题——既给观众一种表达支持的方式,也给 KICK 带来一条建立在真实行为之上、而非生硬嫁接的营收渠道。

SLIDE-02stated-only仅口述
Five proposals in, no one could explain why a viewer would actually want to send one.五版方案过后,没有人能说清楚观众到底为什么会想发送一份礼物。
Content内容
Most features on our roadmap were ones Twitch already had, so the working pattern was to match the mechanic and move on. That was efficient for table-stakes features. It didn't hold here: five proposals went to senior stakeholders and none passed. Each one functioned — buy currency, trigger an animation — but neither the stakeholders nor the design team could say what would make a viewer genuinely want to do it. The project ran well past its timeline, and engineers were blocked waiting on a direction to build. 我们排期上的大多数功能都是 Twitch 已经有的,所以团队惯常的做法是:对齐机制,然后往下走。对于那些"必备功能"来说,这样做很高效。但在这里行不通:五版方案递到高层,全部未获通过。每一版在机制上都能跑通——购买货币、触发动画——但无论高层还是设计团队,都说不清楚究竟是什么会让观众真心想去做这件事。项目早已严重超期,工程师们也在等一个能定稿开发的方向。

GAP-02No description or visual of what the five rejected proposals actually looked like — a glimpse of one would sharpen the before/after contrast.目前没有关于五版被否方案具体样貌的描述或截图——哪怕展示其中一版,也能让"前后对比"更有说服力。
SLIDE-03stated-only仅口述
Instead of a sixth pitch, we asked what would actually make someone want to hit send.与其准备第六版提案,不如先搞清楚到底是什么会让人真心想按下发送键。
Content内容
While the PM renegotiated the delivery timeline, instead of iterating again on the same rejected direction, I pulled the PM and design team into two internal workshops. The goal: find the real behavioral reason someone sends a gift mid-stream — not just refine the payment mechanic. The one constraint we held onto: keep the underlying system familiar, so we weren't adding new cognitive load for viewers already using it. 趁 PM 重新协商交付时间的空档,我没有继续在被否决的方向上打磨,而是拉上 PM 和设计团队开了两场内部工作坊。目标是:找到观众在直播过程中愿意发送礼物的真正行为动机——而不只是优化支付机制本身。我们保留的一条原则是:底层系统要保持熟悉,不给已经在使用的观众增加新的认知负担。
SLIDE-04stated-only仅口述
The real signal wasn't how much someone spent — it was whether the room noticed.真正重要的信号不是花了多少钱,而是这份支持有没有被全场看见。
Content内容
The workshops surfaced the actual driver: viewers didn't send Kicks to feel generous — they sent Kicks to be seen supporting a streamer, in front of the whole chat. That single insight gave the team something concrete to design toward — and to defend to stakeholders — instead of chasing a vaguer goal of "make it feel less like Twitch." 工作坊挖出了真正的驱动力:观众发送 Kicks 并不是为了展现慷慨,而是为了在整个聊天室面前"被看见"自己在支持主播。这一个洞察,给了团队一个具体的设计目标,也让方案有了可以向高层清晰说明和辩护的理由——而不再是含糊地追求"看起来不要太像 Twitch"。

SECTION — from the reframe to the system it produced 章节过渡——从重新定义到落地的系统
SLIDE-05supported有据可查
The system stayed familiar: five tiers, one panel, one glance at your balance.系统本身保持熟悉:五个档位、一个面板、余额一目了然。
Content内容
The Cheer Panel kept a live preview and five preset Kick tiers (1, 100, 1,000, 5,000, 10,000), reachable by clicking the Kicks icon or typing a chat command. Custom amounts snap to the nearest tier. Balance shows in both the top nav and the panel, so viewers always know if they can afford to cheer — or need to top up first. 打赏面板保留了实时预览,以及五个预设的 Kicks 档位(1、100、1,000、5,000、10,000),可通过点击 Kicks 图标或输入聊天指令打开。自定义金额会自动匹配到最接近的档位。余额同时显示在顶部导航栏与面板中,让观众随时清楚自己是否有余额打赏,或是否需要先充值。

SLIDE-07supported有据可查
Web shipped first — so I handed off mobile complete before it was ever scheduled.Web 端先上线——所以我在移动端排期之前,就把它的设计一并交付了。
Content内容
Our pattern was web-first: wireframes for web, approval, then mobile adapted from the approved design — scaled one-to-one, or simplified where the smaller surface demanded it. Most KICK viewers and streamers were on web, and with limited engineering capacity, mobile shipped when availability, feasibility, and timing allowed rather than on a fixed date. I still delivered mobile complete at every handoff, so that whenever mobile got picked up, nobody had to redesign it under pressure. For pinned gift messages that meant mobile matched desktop end to end — same layout, same behavior. 我们的模式是 Web 优先:先做 Web 端线框图,过稿后再由已确认的设计延展到移动端——一比一缩放,或在小屏幕确有必要时做简化。KICK 的观众和主播大多在 Web 端,加上开发人力有限,移动端是在可用性、可行性与时间允许时才灵活上线,并没有固定排期。但我每次交付时都会把移动端设计做完整,这样一旦移动端排上开发,就没有人需要在压力下临时返工重做。以置顶礼物消息为例,移动端与桌面端完全一致——布局相同,行为也相同。
Why this slide为何设为此页
Sets up the constraint that drives SLIDE-08: with a thin engineering team and two surfaces, anything built twice was a risk.为 SLIDE-08 铺设前提:在开发人力紧张、又要覆盖两端的情况下,任何需要做两遍的东西都是风险。
SLIDE-08supported有据可查
Two gift-message designs became one component — and seeing it live told us the tier colors were wrong.两套礼物消息设计合并为一个组件——而在实际流程中一看,才发现档位配色是错的。
Content内容
That same constraint drove the build's biggest revision. Originally the gift message existed as two separate designs. We consolidated them into one component reused across preview, chat, and the pinned card — less to build, easier to maintain through MVP and beyond. Testing the visuals in the live flow then showed the tier-to-color mapping wasn't reading correctly, so we kept the palette and reshuffled which color belonged to which tier. 同样的约束,也带来了开发阶段最大的一次调整。礼物消息最初存在两套独立设计,我们把它们合并为一个组件,在预览、聊天和置顶卡片三处复用——开发量更小,MVP 及后续迭代的维护也更轻。随后在实际流程中测试视觉效果时发现,档位与颜色的对应关系读起来并不对,于是我们保留了原有色板,只重新调整了各档位对应的颜色。

SLIDE-09stated-only仅口述
It shipped mid-timeline, gave viewers a real reason to send, and became the system other Kicks features build on.项目在时间线中段完成交付,给了观众真正想发送的理由,并成为后续 Kicks 相关功能的基础系统。
Content内容
Kicks launched despite starting the redesign already behind schedule. For the first time, viewers had a mechanic that rewarded being seen, not just spending — and it read as genuinely distinct from Twitch's model, which was less the goal than a signal we'd found the real problem. Later features like Goal Setting and Bounties built directly on its Kicks-cost logic and tier language. 尽管重新设计是在项目已经延期的情况下才启动,Kicks 最终还是顺利上线。观众第一次拥有了一种奖励"被看见"而不只是"花钱多少"的机制——它在体验上也确实与 Twitch 的模式明显不同,但这更多是"找对了真正问题"的信号,而不是设计的初衷本身。后续的 Goal Setting、Bounties 等功能都直接沿用了 Kicks 的计费逻辑与档位语言。
SLIDE-10stated-only仅口述
Week one: seven-figure volume — and almost all of it in claps, not patronage.上线第一周:交易额达七位数——但几乎全部是"鼓掌",而非"赞助"。
Content内容
Kicks generated seven-figure transaction volume in its first week. The real insight was in the breakdown: repeat-sending clustered almost entirely on the cheapest tier. Viewers weren't using Kicks as patronage — they were using it as reaction. It confirmed the workshop's read on the behavior, and it changed how the team talked about what to build next: make the cheap, expressive sends feel as good as possible rather than chase high-value ones. Kicks 上线首周便产生了七位数的交易额。真正有价值的洞察藏在分布里:重复发送几乎完全集中在最低档位。观众并不是把 Kicks 当作"赞助",而是当作一种"反应"。这既印证了工作坊对用户行为的判断,也改变了团队后续讨论要做什么的方向:与其追逐高价值打赏,不如把低价、富有表现力的发送体验做到最好。

SLIDE-11stated-only仅口述
What I'd protect next time: argue for measurement as a delivery requirement, not a designer's preference.下次我会守住的一点:把"衡量"当作交付要求去争取,而不是设计师的个人偏好。
Content内容
We had no A/B testing process — design and engineering ran internal QA rounds before and after launch, and post-launch changes were essentially visual bug fixes rather than responses to how people used it. So the week-one insight arrived with nowhere to go. I'd advocated for testing at launch and didn't win it, and I've since concluded I argued it as a designer's preference instead of as the thing that decides whether the next feature is a guess. Two things I'd hold onto: run the behavioral workshop before five rounds of rejection, and agree what we're measuring — and how we'll act on it — before anything ships. 我们当时没有 A/B testing 流程——设计和开发在上线前后各做一轮内部 QA,而上线后的改动基本上是修复视觉 bug,而不是针对用户实际使用方式做出的调整。所以首周那个洞察出现时,它其实无处可去。我曾争取过上线时做测试,但没有争取成功;现在回头看,我意识到自己当时是把它当作"设计师的偏好"来讲的,而不是把它讲成"决定下一个功能是不是在靠猜"的关键。有两件事我会一直坚持:把探究用户行为的工作坊放在五轮被否决之前做,以及在任何东西上线之前,就先谈妥我们要衡量什么、拿到结果后要怎么行动。
