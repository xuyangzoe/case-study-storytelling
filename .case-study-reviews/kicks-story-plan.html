Story contract故事契约

Central question核心问题
What do you do when eight versions of a feature have failed review and the problem isn't the screens — it's that you can't explain why any of them are right? 当一个功能的八个版本都没能通过评审,而问题并不出在界面上——而是你说不清它们凭什么是对的——这时候该怎么办?
Recommended argument推荐的故事主张
Faithfully executing a PRD produced eight versions I couldn't defend; only when I stopped designing and answered the question I'd skipped — what does a viewer actually get for this? — did the system resolve into tiered gifting built around being seen, which shipped, launched to real volume, and showed that KICK's engagement currency was visibility, not value. 忠实执行 PRD 换来的是八个我无法为之辩护的版本;直到我停下设计、回答那个被我跳过的问题——观众到底能从中得到什么?——整套系统才收敛成一个围绕"被看见"构建的分档送礼机制。它成功上线、产生了真实交易量,也证明了 KICK 真正的参与货币是被看见,而不是花费的金额。

Kicks — KICK's Cheer & Gifting SystemKicks——KICK 平台的打赏与送礼系统

KICK's answer to Twitch's Bits — a virtual currency viewers buy and send to support streamers live. KICK 对标 Twitch Bits 的答案——一种观众可购买、用于实时支持主播的虚拟货币。

Role: Product Designer, solo — owned design end-to-end across web and mobile · Hero artifact: the shipped Cheer Panel (see ASSET-01) 角色:产品设计师,独立负责——端到端主导 Web 与移动端设计 · 主视觉素材:上线版打赏面板(见 ASSET-01)
SLIDE-01stated-only仅口述
Viewers had no real-time way to show they backed a streamer — and KICK had no monetization engine built around that moment.观众没有实时的方式表达自己支持某位主播——而 KICK 也没有围绕这一时刻建立起变现机制。
Content内容
On KICK, viewers react to live moments in real time but had no lightweight way to make that support visible to the streamer or the room. For the business, that was also a gap: no revenue mechanic tied to genuine, in-the-moment engagement. Kicks set out to solve both at once — give viewers a way to cheer, and give KICK a new revenue stream built on real behavior, not a bolted-on payment button. 在 KICK 上,观众会对直播中的精彩时刻实时产生情绪反应,却没有一种轻量的方式,把这份支持可见地表达给主播和聊天室。对业务来说,这同样是一个缺口:没有与真实、即时参与绑定的营收机制。Kicks 想要同时解决这两个问题——既给观众一种表达支持的方式,也给 KICK 带来一条建立在真实行为之上、而非生硬嫁接的营收渠道。

Content内容
The PRD asked for Twitch's Cheering with Bits, so that's what I built: the same mechanic, the same logic, with the UI reworked — tiers laid out three per row, and a tap animation that popped "+1" as you added a Kick. I took the low-fi prototype to my mentor and design lead, then to the design head and chief creative officer. All four said it was fine to keep going. The notes were font sizes and spacing. PRD 要求对齐 Twitch 的 Cheering with Bits,我就照着做了:机制和逻辑完全一致,只重做了 UI——档位改成每行三个,并加入点击动效,加一个 Kick 时弹出"+1"。我把低保真原型先后拿给 mentor 和 design lead 看,再给 design head 和首席创意官看。四个人都说可以继续推进,反馈集中在字号和间距上。

I could execute a PRD, but I couldn't say why any of it was right — so every review failed on the same point, and no number of new drafts fixed it. What changed the outcome was stopping to define the behavior we were designing for, in language I could hold up under questioning. I now treat that as the first deliverable: if I can't explain in one sentence why a design is right, it isn't ready — however finished it looks. 这个项目差点翻船的原因,是我产出的速度超过了我理解的速度。我能执行 PRD,却说不出其中任何一处凭什么是对的——所以每次评审都栽在同一个点上,再多的新稿也解决不了。真正改变结果的,是我停下来,把我们究竟在为什么样的行为做设计定义清楚,并且用经得起追问的语言表述出来。现在我把这件事当作第一交付物:如果我没法用一句话说清一个设计为什么是对的,那它就还没准备好——不管它看上去多完整。
